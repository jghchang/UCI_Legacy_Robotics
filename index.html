Our Mission

Legacy Robotics is a student-run team operating out of UC Irvine, Samueli School of Engineering. We promote the concepts of STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics) through building robots. We aim to inspire innovation and teamwork throught the application of cutting-edge technology, foster hands-on engineering expreience with practical projects, and cultivate a passion for engineering among students.

Our Goals
  1. Fabricate a fully-functional rover by Spring 2027
  2. Iterate and improve on design in time for our Winter Design Review 2027
  3. Submit our rover for the Systems Acceptance Review (SAR) and compete in the Canadian Rover challenge and the University Rover Challenge (UCR) 2027!
